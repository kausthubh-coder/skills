# Naming

My taste, not a rule: I often name projects with short Sanskrit words people don't already use (krida = play, kriyan). Any good name is fine.

- Short (2-3 syllables), easy to say and spell after hearing it once.
- Means something about the product.
- Not already a popular app, package or company.
- If Sanskrit: everyday words, no diacritics, nothing sacred (deities, rituals, mantras).

Offer a mix: a few Sanskrit options and a few others, each with one line on what it means and why it fits. Mark your favorite.
